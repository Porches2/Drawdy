FAQ

Good questions

Is Drawdy free?+

Yes — personal canvases are free forever, with no limits on drawings. Team workspaces with shared libraries and history start on a paid plan.

How is it different from Excalidraw?+

We love Excalidraw. Drawdy focuses on builders: live objects instead of flattened strokes, first-class architecture and flow-mapping primitives, and multiplayer designed for standups, not just sessions.

Can I export my work?+

Export to SVG, PNG, or Drawdy's open JSON format at any time. Your canvases are yours — copy them out whenever you want.

Where is my work stored?+

On your device by default. Drawdy is local-first — draw fully offline, nothing leaves your machine. Turn on cloud sync per board to access other devices and collaborate.
