Introducing Drawdy: The Infinite Canvas for Builders

Jul 22, 2026 · Alex Kim · 2 min read

We’re excited to introduce Drawdy — an infinite canvas built from scratch for people who think in systems.

Whiteboards are great for brainstorming, but they fall apart when you need structure. Design tools are beautiful, but they weren’t made for architecture diagrams or technical planning. Code editors are powerful, but they can’t show the big picture.

Drawdy lives in the space between all three.

Why we built this

Every builder we talked to had the same frustration: they’d start on a whiteboard, move to a doc, then to a design tool, then to code — losing context at every handoff. The thinking that happened on the whiteboard never made it to production.

We wanted to build a tool where your architectural thinking and your implementation could live in the same place.

What makes Drawdy different

  • Infinite canvas with real structure. Not just sticky notes — you can create nodes, connectors, and diagrams that actually mean something.
  • Local-first by default. Your data lives on your device. No account required to start. No cloud dependency.
  • Built for speed. Drawdy is fast. Really fast. We obsess over frame rates and input latency because creative tools should never make you wait.
  • Extensible. A plugin API that lets you build custom nodes, integrations, and workflows on top of the canvas.

What’s next

We’re shipping fast. In the coming weeks, you’ll see real-time collaboration, version history, and our first set of community plugins. Follow along on this blog — we’ll share the technical decisions, design philosophy, and roadmap as we build in the open.

Welcome to Drawdy.
