From Excalidraw to Drawdy: What's Different

Jun 30, 2026 · Alex Kim · 2 min read

We love Excalidraw. It proved that a web-based whiteboard could be fast, beautiful, and open source. It inspired a generation of canvas tools, and it showed that hand-drawn aesthetics aren’t just charming — they reduce the pressure to make things “perfect” and let you focus on thinking.

Drawdy takes that spirit and pushes it further.

What we share

Both tools believe in:

  • Hand-drawn aesthetics. Sketchy lines, imperfect shapes, the feeling of paper. This isn’t decoration — it’s a design decision that encourages exploration over polish.
  • Speed. A creative tool that makes you wait isn’t a creative tool.
  • Simplicity. The best tools feel obvious. No 200-button toolbars.

Where Drawdy diverges

Structure beyond shapes

Excalidraw gives you shapes and freeform drawing. Drawdy gives you that plus structured elements: typed nodes, connectors with labels, grouping, and layout helpers. Think of it as the difference between a sketchpad and an architecture workbench.

Local-first with sync

Excalidraw stores boards in browser localStorage or exports to files. Drawdy uses a CRDT-based local store with optional cloud sync. Your data persists across sessions, syncs across devices when you want, and works offline by default.

Plugin ecosystem

Drawdy ships with a plugin API from day one. Custom node types, integrations with external tools, workflow automations — the canvas is a platform, not just a drawing surface.

Individual post pages (like this one)

Excalidraw is a tool; Drawdy is building a product. That means a landing page, blog, documentation, community, and a clear roadmap. We’re here for the long term.

Should you switch?

If Excalidraw does everything you need, keep using it. It’s a great tool.

But if you’ve ever wished your whiteboard could handle more structure, persist your work reliably, or integrate with your development workflow — Drawdy might be what you’re looking for.

Try it and let us know.
